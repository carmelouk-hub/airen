# RISTOAIREN

RISTOAIREN is the AIRenOS hospitality specialization and an entitled consumer of autonomous AIRenOS capabilities.

AIRen Booking is owned by `packages/booking-core`. AIRenPay is owned by `packages/airenpay`. The matching paths below `packages/ristoairen/src` are compatibility re-export adapters only; they do not own or fork either capability.

Hospitality-specific mappings, table/cover/service semantics and commercial packaging may remain here. Provider-neutral Booking or AIRenPay product logic may not.
